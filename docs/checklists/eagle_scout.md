# Eagle Scout






## After Eagle Project and Other Requirements are complete

Next steps! Please read carefully.

Before an Eagle Board of Review can be scheduled, the Commitee Chair and the Advancement Coordinator need to submit your required paperwork to Council for them to review. It is best to neatly organize the documents below in a binder.

The three items that need to be in your binder:
1) Eagle Scout Rank ApplicationCompleted 
2) Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook
3) Statement of ambitions and life purpose, and listing of positions, honors, and awards.

Eagle Scout Rank Application
You can print this from Scoutbook at any time (go to Scoutbook > Reports > Eagle Scout Application). It has been preloaded with some information from Scoutbook. 

However, please take a look and fill in the blank areas. Scoutbook does not fill in everything. 
The Date Project Finished is auto-filled from Scoutbook. Make sure you update the date should be the same date as the approvals signatures in the Project Report section of your Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook.

Please double check that the Grand Total of Hours on the application, matches what is in your Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook.

Please double check all dates: DOB, BOR Dates, Merit Badge Dates, Leadership Dates. You can do this by using Scoutbook, Internet Advancement, Blue Cards, or your Scout Handbook.

References -You need to provide the information for four people.
You will be responsible for requesting the references from those four people, however, they will submit them directly to Committee Chair (or in a sealed envelope). 

I will provide you with sample reference request letters that you can send to your references and additional information on this step, when you are ready to start this process. 

The application needs to be signed by:
You, Your Unit Leader (Scoutmaster ),Committee Chair 

Note: You complete the application up to the 3 signatures on page two of the application. You do not need to fill in any information, from “BSA Local Council Verification” or beyond.

2. Completed Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook
This should be the official Eagle Scout Service Project Workbook and include your:Eagle Scout Service Project Proposal, Eagle Scout Service Project Plan, Eagle Scout Service Project Report, Everything should be filled out and if a section does not apply to you, then you can make note of that. If you have photos, those are great to add too.

3.  Statement of ambitions and life purpose, and listing of positions, honors, and awards.
See Requirement #6 on the Eagle Scout Application, "In preparation for your board of review, prepare and attach to your Eagle Scout Rank Application a statement of your ambitions and life purpose and a listing of positions held in your religious institution, school, camp, community, or other organizations, during which you demonstrated leadership skills. Include honors and awards received during this service."

It's a lot of information. Please at least 3x. If you have any questions let me know.

## Eagle Court of Honor

The Eagle Court of Honor is the responsibility of the family. The family schedules it, sends out the invites, recruits other scouts to participate in the program. The troop is happy to help and lend support, just not lead.

If a scout does not want to have an Eagle Court of Honor, we can recognize the scout in the quarterly court of honor.
